Bug Summary

  1. Appointment Module – Daily Sequence
     Problem:
     The therapist availability check is wrong. The system is marking therapists as unavailable even when they actually have a valid schedule and open time.

  What this likely means:

  - Assigned therapist schedules are not being read correctly.
  - Existing appointments may be blocking full availability instead of only overlapping slots.
  - Time-slot overlap validation is too broad or incorrect.
  - Real-time schedule and booking data may not be syncing properly.

  Impact:

  - Valid therapists do not appear available.
  - Appointment allocation becomes inaccurate.
  - Daily sequence planning is unreliable.

  2. Automated SMS Reminder – Pre-Appointment
     Problem:
     Patients are not getting the reminder SMS 30 minutes before their appointment.

  What this likely means:

  - No scheduled cron/queue job is running, or it is misconfigured.
  - Appointment time and current time comparison may be failing.
  - Time-zone conversion may be incorrect.
  - Patient mobile numbers may not be fetched properly.
  - SMS failures may happen silently without retry.

  Impact:

  - Patients miss reminders.
  - Higher chance of no-shows or delayed attendance.
  - Reminder automation is not functioning as expected.

  3. Therapy Completion – Bulk Auto-Marking
     Problem:
     When one patient is marked as completed, multiple other patients are also getting marked completed by mistake.

  What this likely means:

  - The completion action is affecting a list instead of one selected record.
  - There may be a loop or bulk update running unintentionally.
  - Patient/session IDs may be mixed up or not uniquely scoped.
  - UI selection or event binding may be targeting multiple rows.

  Impact:

  - Incorrect therapy records are updated.
  - Patient treatment status becomes unreliable.
  - Risk of serious data integrity issues.

  4. Appointment Notification Message Failure
     Problem:
     The system is not sending confirmation/notification messages when an appointment is created.

  What this likely means:

  - SMS gateway/API integration may be broken or misconfigured.
  - Notification trigger may not be firing after successful appointment creation.
  - API request/response handling may be failing.
  - There may be no proper logging for failures.

  Impact:

  - Patients do not receive booking confirmations.
  - Communication flow after appointment creation is broken.
  - Troubleshooting is difficult without logs.

  Overall Pattern
  The main problems fall into 3 categories:

  - Incorrect business logic: therapist availability and therapy completion
  - Automation failure: pre-appointment reminder job not running properly
  - Notification integration failure: booking confirmation SMS not being triggered or delivered
